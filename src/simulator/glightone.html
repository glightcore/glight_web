GLIGHTONE · RC2 CONTROLS

Use the OEM panel for vehicle signals. MODE and DRL_SW operate the module. A click is resolved after the 350 ms multi-click window.

ContextMODE
Normal1 click: DRL mode
2 clicks: EXP mode
3 clicks: EMG mode / OFF
Hold 800 ms: next Welcome
DIR active1 click: sequence
Hold 800 ms: speed
STOP active1 click: STOP mode
REV active1 click: REV mode

Context priority: REV → DIR → STOP → normal. A gesture keeps the context in which it started. Holds in STOP / REV do not change modes.

DIR input pulses are bridged by the RC2 650 ms hold. Local DIR overrides DRL and cancels Welcome. POS dims authorized DRL and EXP, and supplies the tail position level. STOP and REV also work during Welcome.

Wireframe brightness, fixture color and EMG location are visualization choices, not firmware settings. Rear fog, side markers and red eyes share the same EMG output. Switching fixture selects a starting color; EMG color can then be customized. POS halos share EXP. The light-group buttons and light tour show placement only. Click a selected group again or Clear to turn its preview off. Choose Live to return to OEM operation. Clear does not reset the module modes or OEM switches. Use the Wireframe slider to dim the car independently. The light rendering boosts dim outputs for visibility without changing their simulated PWM.
Output behavior model based on the supplied RC2. Browser timing, brightness and paired-module coordination are illustrative. Physical SYNC pulses, TFT menus, EEPROM and electrical characteristics are not emulated. The original INO is unchanged.
